> Week 1:

Learned basics of aerial motion with quadcopters:

- Pitch: Forwards/Backwards tilt to cause movement

- Roll: Left/Right tilt to cause movement

- Yaw: Stationary rotation

- All forms of movement created by manipulating the strengths of motors (Decrease front motors speed to move forward etc.)

Learned general important vocabulary:

- Lift: Force needed to counteract weight (Wings on an airplane, propellors on a quadcopter)

- Weight: Downward force created by the mass of the object

- Thrust: Force created by aerial vehicles to move (usually forward)

- Drag: Resisting force that thrust must overcome, increases roughly with (speed)^2

  - Speed x2 ~ drag x4

<!-- -->

- Thrust to weight ratio: Needs to be at least 1.0 for a quadcopter to hover and over 1.0 to take off (Planes are around 0.3 as the wings provide lift unlike on a quadcopter)

Very basic layout learning (Expand next week):

- Motors form a square shape with the body of the quadcopter in the middle

- Diagonally opposite motors spin the same direction (2 clockwise, 2 counterclockwise to balance torque)
